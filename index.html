Jezki — from ježko, Slavic for “little hedgehog.” ● It gathers, then guards.

How Jezki gathers

Proof comes in messy. Jezki keeps it calm.

It keeps the original evidence, suggests useful fields, and waits for your review before anything becomes a record.

1

Gather

Add proof from camera, document scan, Photos, Files, the share sheet, or by hand. Jezki keeps the original.

2

Sniff out fields

Where it can, Jezki suggests likely fields and a category. Suggestions, never decisions.

3

You review

Every possible record waits in review. Confirm, edit, merge a possible duplicate, or dismiss it.

4

Tuck it away

Local-First By Default

It curls up around your evidence.

A hedgehog’s first instinct is to protect what it carries. Jezki keeps your evidence on device by default, waits for your approval before filing anything, and only lets a pack leave when you send it.

  • Local-first by defaultCurrent builds do not upload expense evidence to a Jezki backend by default.
  • You choose the sourcesNothing is captured unless you add it. Original evidence stays connected to the record you approve.
  • Review before recordsPossible records enter review. Nothing is filed until you approve it.
  • No tracking or advertisingNo third-party advertising or tracking. Exports and shares are user-initiated.

Accountant-ready exports

When it’s time, hand over a tidy pack.

Jezki can build an accountant-ready export pack from the records you approved — reviewed fields, a readable summary, a manifest, and the original evidence, bundled together.

  • CSV Reviewed records and fields
  • PDF A readable summary
  • Manifest What is inside the pack
  • Bundle The original evidence you approved

Where Jezki stops

Jezki does not provide tax advice, decide deductibility, guarantee savings, take the place of an accountant, or file records on its own.

It organises evidence so a person can review it. Jezki suggests useful fields; you decide what becomes a record. It does not claim approval from any tax authority.
