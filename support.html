What Jezki handles

Jezki organises user-selected receipts, invoices, PDFs, screenshots, manually entered expense evidence, selected Photos and Files, shared items, and local export packs. When enabled, it can also organise optional Gmail test-source data.

Every source path is something you choose. Jezki saves the original evidence, suggests fields where it can, and creates a possible record for you to review.

Deleting local data

Open the Privacy tab in Jezki to manage your local data. From there you can review local counts, create a data inventory export, and delete local Jezki records, candidates, source state, export metadata, preferences, and evidence files, where they can be moved to pending purge.

Deletion is a local action under your control. If you want a record of what exists before deleting, create a data inventory export first.

Gmail & source controls

Current builds use Gmail test mode only. Production Gmail remains unavailable until Google OAuth verification, security review, final privacy copy, and owner approval are complete.

If Gmail is enabled in a future verified build, it must stay optional, transparent, pausable, disconnectable, and review-based. Gmail findings become candidates for review — they are never silently filed records; they wait for your approval like any other source.

Exporting records

Use the Exports tab to create accountant-ready export packs from records you have already reviewed and approved. A pack can include a CSV of reviewed fields, a PDF summary, a manifest, and an evidence bundle for the date range and records you select.

Exports are user-initiated. Nothing leaves your device unless you create a pack and choose to share it.

What Jezki does not do

Jezki organises evidence and suggests review fields. It does not provide tax advice, validate records with tax authorities, decide what can be deducted, guarantee savings, or replace professional advice.

For anything that depends on a final support contact, please wait for the owner-approved destination. There are no promised response times, service-level agreements, or live support channels yet.
