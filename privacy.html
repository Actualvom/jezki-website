Jezki Privacy Policy

Jezki handles private financial evidence. The model is local-first, consent-led, and review-first. In plain language: you choose what goes in, it stays on your device by default, and nothing leaves unless you send it.

Summary

Current builds are local-first and do not upload expense evidence to a Jezki backend by default. Jezki does not send evidence away by default; exports and shares are user-initiated. Jezki does not use your data for third-party advertising or tracking.

Local-First Storage

Jezki stores expense evidence files and its metadata locally on your device. The core app is designed to work without an account, backend service, cloud OCR provider, subscription, or mandatory Gmail connection.

Data You Choose To Add

Jezki processes only what you choose to give it. That can include receipts, invoices, PDFs, screenshots, manually entered records, selected Photos and Files, camera captures, document scans, and share-sheet items. Optional Gmail source data is processed only when an eligible Gmail mode is explicitly enabled.

Alongside your evidence, Jezki keeps related metadata such as merchant names, dates, amounts, tax details, tags, notes, suggested categories, and review state. This metadata is stored locally with your records.

Review Before Records

Extracted fields are treated as suggestions for review. Possible records must be reviewed and approved by you before they become saved records. Jezki does not silently file findings — a possible record waits for your decision.

No Tracking Or Advertising

Jezki does not use your personal data for third-party advertising or tracking. The current app does not require an account and does not include third-party advertising, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Gmail & Source Controls

Current builds expose Gmail test mode only. Production Gmail access must remain unavailable unless Google OAuth verification, any required security review, final privacy language, and owner approval are complete.

Every source makes its state understandable and, where applicable, supports pause, disconnect, and delete. All source output enters review before approval.

App Lock

Optional app lock uses device owner authentication through iOS, such as Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode. Jezki does not collect biometric templates or passcode values.

Exports & Sharing

You can create and share export packs. Exported CSV, PDF summary, manifest, evidence bundle, and data inventory files may contain private financial evidence and should be shared carefully. Because exports and shares are user-initiated, this is the point where data can leave your device — only when you choose.

Deletion Controls

The Privacy tab provides local deletion controls for Jezki records, candidates, source state, export metadata, preferences, and evidence files, where they can be moved to pending purge. Gmail test-source data has separate local deletion controls.

Future Changes

Any future sync, cloud OCR, accounts, subscriptions, production Gmail release, analytics, crash reporting, third-party SDK, or backend feature will require a privacy re-audit and must be reflected in this policy before it is released.
